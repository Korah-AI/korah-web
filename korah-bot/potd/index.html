One new problem every day

Every day you get a single SAT problem to work through. It takes a couple of minutes, and it is the same problem everyone else is solving that day.

Solve it yourself first

Work the problem out on your own, pick or type the answer you landed on, and hit Submit. You find out right away whether you got it.

Then compare with the walkthrough

Submitting unlocks the walkthrough video. Watch how the problem is solved and compare it to your own method, whether you got it right or not.
