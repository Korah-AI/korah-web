What Korah CODE is

What is the Korah CODE internship?

AI slop. Vibecoding. Ever heard of it? In the modern era, too many people are given the most powerful tools to create beautfiul software, but not many truly know what they're doing. Korah CODE is meant to teach the coming generation of programmers the art of Agentic Programming: how to use AI to assist you in producing actually good code, not slop.

During this internship, you'll get to contribute to a real-world, impactful codebase in many creative ways. I will personally mentor you and give you advice on how to apply the skills you learn in a way that benefits you exponentially (like making a passion project or softwares that bring profit.) You'll be tasked with two major projects that will be actually used by users, and given various lessons and assignments throughout your internship. Be prepared to be challenged with real tasks, collaborate with your team to complete assignments, and spend time designing your own creative solutions to given problems.

Only a handful of applicants will be selected for this internship. All programming will be done using HTML, CSS, and JS. The selection criteria is relatively light: experience with HTML/CSS/JS and experience with web development. Applicants are highly encouraged to provide their resume, Github username, or digital portfolio.
